Zone B · Relational efficacy

The same function, eight different animals

Fi has no fixed identity — its depth, steadiness, and honesty depend on where it sits in a type's cognitive stack. Choose a depth and watch the nebula weigh incoming experience: what rings true diffuses into the mist with a soft green light and sets it turning; what rings false cuts straight through — the mist churns around its path, and the chamber bleeds a little where it exits. The deeper the position, the more often the weighing itself goes wrong. Below the waterline lies the shadow register: positions Fi occupies unconsciously.

Zone D · Verification

When the world strikes the fork

Fi doesn't argue — it weighs. Every experience is struck against the core tone, and the nebula answers with fluid, not logic. Authentic experience — joyful or painful — diffuses into the mist and colours it. Inauthentic experience never mixes: the pleasant kind glides off unabsorbed, and the violating kind tears straight through, dragging a vacuum and bleeding mist through the wound.

The nebula turns slowly at baseline. Spawn an experience and watch Fi do what Fi actually does all day: weigh.

Zone F · Field notes

Fi among the others

Fi ↔ Fe

Same element, opposite attitude. Fi refines an internal tone until it is true; Fe tunes the room until it is warm. Fi asks "can I live with this?" — Fe asks "what does everyone need?" Each quietly suspects the other of missing the point of feeling.

Fi at a dinner party

Quiet through an hour of gossip, then one soft "that isn't fair" that stops the table — followed by genuine surprise at having said it out loud.

Fi choosing work

Cannot fake enthusiasm, and doesn't understand how anyone can. A project that violates the tone gets quietly, completely abandoned — whatever it pays.

Fi in an argument

Doesn't want to win — wants to be understood. Goes silent rather than lie, concedes every fact freely, and never, ever the principle.
