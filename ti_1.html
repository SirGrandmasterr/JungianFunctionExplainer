Zone B · Relational efficacy

The same function, eight different animals

Ti has no fixed identity — its power, speed, and reliability depend on where it sits in a type's cognitive stack. Choose a depth and watch the lattice judge incoming claims: structures that fit its logic fold in with a green pulse; structures that don't bounce off with a red one. The deeper the position, the more often the judgment itself goes wrong. Below the waterline lies the shadow register: positions Ti occupies unconsciously.

Zone D · Verification

When the world talks back

Ti doesn't only build — it checks. Every observation arrives as a small structure that must be reconciled with the lattice. Spawn one and watch what it does to the chamber's stress and pleasure: unrelated facts park peacefully, causal connections integrate joyfully, and rule violations force the lattice to tear itself apart and reorder around the exception.

The lattice hums along at baseline. Spawn an observation and watch Ti do what Ti actually does all day: verify.

Zone F · Field notes

Ti among the others

Ti ↔ Te

Same element, opposite attitude. Ti refines an internal lattice until it is coherent; Te assembles an external scaffold until it works. Ti asks "is this true?" — Te asks "does this ship?" Each finds the other's question slightly beside the point.

Ti at a dinner party

Quiet for an hour, then one sentence that reorganizes the whole debate — followed by mild surprise that anyone found it controversial.

Ti debugging

Refuses the quick patch. Will not rest until the actual cause is isolated, named, and slotted into the model of how the system really works.

Ti in an argument

Attacks the structure, never the person — and is baffled when the person feels attacked anyway. Concedes instantly, and completely, to the better framework.
